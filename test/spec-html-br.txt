# Inline HTML line breaks

Only the three exact spellings are opt-in hard breaks.

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br>two<br/>three<br />four<BR>five<Br/>six<bR />seven
.
<p>one<br>
two<br>
three<br>
four<br>
five<br>
six<br>
seven</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br>two
.
<p>one&lt;br&gt;two</p>
.
--fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
| cell |
| --- |
| one<br>two |
.
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>cell</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>one<br>
two</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html --ftables
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
`one<br>two` and \<br> and &lt;br&gt;
.
<p><code>one&lt;br&gt;two</code> and &lt;br&gt; and &lt;br&gt;</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br >two<br  />three<br x>four</br>five<br/ >six
.
<p>one&lt;br &gt;two&lt;br  /&gt;three&lt;br x&gt;four&lt;/br&gt;five&lt;br/ &gt;six</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

## HTML policy flags

The extension works with each raw-HTML policy. XHTML output also exercises
the existing hard-break renderer rather than passing the input tag through.

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br>two<br/>three<br />four
.
<p>one<br />
two<br />
three<br />
four</p>
.
--fhtml-br --xhtml
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br>two<br/>three<br />four
.
<p>one<br />
two<br />
three<br />
four</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html-spans --xhtml
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br>two<br/>three<br />four
.
<p>one<br />
two<br />
three<br />
four</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html-blocks --xhtml
````````````````````````````````

Without the flag, raw inline HTML retains its original spelling.

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br>two<br/>three<br />four
.
<p>one<br>two<br/>three<br />four</p>
.
--xhtml
````````````````````````````````

## Formatting and literal controls

```````````````````````````````` example
**one**<br><br/>[two](/link)<BR />three
.
<p><strong>one</strong><br>
<br>
<a href="/link">two</a><br>
three</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
one<br title="<br>">two<span title="<br/>">three<!-- <br> -->four<?test <br> ?>five<![CDATA[<br>]]>six
.
<p>one&lt;br title=&quot;&lt;br&gt;&quot;&gt;two&lt;span title=&quot;&lt;br/&gt;&quot;&gt;three&lt;!-- &lt;br&gt; --&gt;four&lt;?test &lt;br&gt; ?&gt;five&lt;![CDATA[&lt;br&gt;]]&gt;six</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
```html
one<br>two<br/>three<br />four
```
.
<pre><code class="language-html">one&lt;br&gt;two&lt;br/&gt;three&lt;br /&gt;four
</code></pre>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
    one<br>two
.
<pre><code>one&lt;br&gt;two
</code></pre>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
<br@example.org> and <https://example.org> and one<b>two</b>
.
<p><a href="mailto:br@example.org">br@example.org</a> and <a href="https://example.org">https://example.org</a> and one&lt;b&gt;two&lt;/b&gt;</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
one<bravo>two<br/ >three<br
four<br/
.
<p>one&lt;bravo&gt;two&lt;br/ &gt;three&lt;br
four&lt;br/</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html
````````````````````````````````

## HTML blocks

HTML block classification takes precedence over the inline extension.

```````````````````````````````` example
<br>
.
<br>
.
--fhtml-br
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
<br>
.
<p><br>
</p>
.
--fhtml-br --fno-html-blocks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
<div>
one<br>two
</div>
.
<div>
one<br>two
</div>
.
--fhtml-br
````````````````````````````````
